About the mentor

A classroom built around understanding

I started Indranil Classes about seven years ago in Bisharpara with a small batch and one belief: a student who understands a concept does not need to memorise it. My own schooling and my years at IIT Guwahati taught me that the toughest problems become simple once the basics are clear.

Today the classes cover Physics and Mathematics from Class VIII to XII, along with JEE, NEET and IISER preparation. I still check every student's progress myself, and every parent is welcome to walk in and talk to me.

  • Taught by an IIT Guwahati engineer, not a rotating set of tutors
  • Daily Practice Problems and a test every week
  • Handwritten notes prepared for each topic
  • Doubt-clearing sessions and individual support
  • Career guidance for engineering, medical and research paths
